New & Interactive Project

Animal Tales Interactive Platform

An intelligent storytelling web application featuring rich illustrated animal stories, interactive choices, voice narration, and educational quizzes for children.

Engaging Interactive Storytelling

The platform delivers a delightful digital reading experience combining vibrant artwork, ambient soundscapes, character voiceovers, and interactive story paths where young readers make choices to guide character moral decisions.

Voice Narration & Audio FX

Full voiceover support with dynamic audio narration, wilderness ambient soundscapes, and animal sound effects that immerse young readers.

Interactive Branching Paths

Branching story choices allow kids to decide moral actions for animal characters, boosting critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

Educational Quizzes & Moral Lessons

End-of-story quizzes reinforce moral values, reading comprehension, and fun scientific facts about wildlife and animal habitats.

Digital Canvas & Coloring Studio

An integrated coloring studio enables kids to color story illustrations and save their custom artistic creations.
