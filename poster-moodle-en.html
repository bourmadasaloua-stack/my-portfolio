University Moodle Access Request System

Moodle Account System

An advanced university portal enabling students to submit access requests to create accounts on the Moodle distance learning platform.

Automated Student Registration Management

The platform simplifies receiving student credentials (name, birthdate, registration code, faculty, department), validating details automatically, and compiling Moodle-compatible batch files to expedite university digital transformation.

Smart Registration Forms

Interactive student input screens with immediate structural validation for enrollment registration numbers.

Automatic Department Routing

Sorts submitted requests by college department, routing them to specific department administrators for faster independent approvals.

Moodle Batch Creation

Exports verified credentials into Moodle CSV formats, allowing university sysadmins to upload and activate thousands of student profiles in seconds.

Student Notifications

Updates students on their request approval state and automatically delivers login credentials upon profile activation.
